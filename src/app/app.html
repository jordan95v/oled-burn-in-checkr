❓ How to use ?

This application is straightforward to use. Just follow these steps:

  • Choose a color from the card on the right (or below on smaller screens).
  • You can cycle through the colors using the ← and → keys.

You can also use the F11 key to toggle fullscreen mode in most browsers.
